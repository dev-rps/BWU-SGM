# 📍 TomTom Routing, Traffic & Search APIs

Safety Guardian leverages three TomTom services:
1. **Routing API** (`api.tomtom.com/routing/1/calculateRoute`): Multi-alternative route generator with traffic delay categories.
2. **Traffic Flow & Incident Tiles** (`api.tomtom.com/traffic/map/4/tile`): Raster overlays for congestion and accidents.
3. **Places & Search API** (`api.tomtom.com/search/2/search`): POI search and address lookups across India.
