# 🧩 MainLayout (`MainLayout.jsx`)

- **Path**: `src/components/navigation/MainLayout.jsx`
- **Role**: Persistent bottom navigation bar wrapping all authenticated routes.
- **Tabs**:
  1. **Home** (`/`): Map overview, SOS button, weather, safety score.
  2. **Journey** (`/routes`): Route selector and navigation.
  3. **Safety** (`/safety`): Safety score breakdown and emergency amenities.
  4. **Reports** (`/reports`): Community hazard report feed and submission.
  5. **Momo Chat** (`/chat`): AI safety assistant.
  6. **Profile** (`/profile`): Account, emergency contacts, medical records.
