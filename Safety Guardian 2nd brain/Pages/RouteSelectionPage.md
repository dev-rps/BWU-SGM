# 📱 RouteSelectionPage (`RouteSelectionPage.jsx`)

- **Route**: `/routes`
- **Key Modules**:
  - **3-State Collapsible Bottom Sheet**: Collapsed Peek, Half Comparison, Expanded Details.
  - **Multi-Route Layered Polyline Canvas**: White-cased primary and secondary candidate routes.
  - **Via-Roads Badges**: Shows exact physical road corridors (`via Jessore Road / NH 112`).
  - **Safety Breakdown Accordion**
  - **Hazard Layer Toggles**: Floating chips above the map (`Crime`, `Disaster & Flood`, `Accidents`, `Traffic`) allowing the user to filter specific risk circles. Both `showDisasters` and `showFloodRisk` are synchronized to prevent orphaned risk layer rendering.
  - **Mode Selector**: Driving, Walking, Cycling.
