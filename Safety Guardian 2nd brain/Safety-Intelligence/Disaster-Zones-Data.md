# 🌋 Disaster Zones Intelligence (`disasterZones.js`)

`src/data/disasterZones.js` catalogs recurring disaster-prone zones in India, categorized by hazard type:

1. **Cyclones & Coastal Surges**: Bay of Bengal & Arabian Sea coastal belts (e.g. Digha, Sundarbans, Puri).
2. **Seismic & Earthquake Zones**: Zone IV and Zone V Himalayan and tectonic rift zones.
3. **Landslide Corridors**: NH-10 (Siliguri–Sikkim), Western Ghats, and Jammu–Srinagar highway.
4. **Extreme Heatwaves**: Central and North Indian summer heat pockets ($>45^\circ\text{C}$).

---

## 🛡️ Route Impact
When a route crosses within $500\text{ meters}$ of an active disaster zone, the navigation card alerts the user with immediate NDMA helpline numbers (`1078`) and evacuation recommendations.
