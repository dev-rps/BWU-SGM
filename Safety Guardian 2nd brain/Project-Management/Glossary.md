# 📖 Glossary of Terms

- **AQI**: Air Quality Index (measures PM2.5, PM10, ozone, and particulate matter).
- **Blackspot**: A recurring motor vehicle collision hotspot identified by highway authorities.
- **Haversine Formula**: Mathematical formula determining great-circle distances between points on a sphere.
- **Momo**: Safety Guardian's offline-first multilingual conversational AI assistant.
- **OSRM**: Open Source Routing Machine, a high-performance open-source routing engine based on OpenStreetMap.
- **TwiML**: Twilio Markup Language (XML) instructing voice servers how to handle phone calls.
- **Zustand**: Fast, lightweight state management library for React.
